Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

How do offline downloads work? −

Pro tier: Download up to 50 meditations per month. Premium tier: Download up to 200 per month. Once downloaded, you can play them anytime without internet. Perfect for workouts, travel, or airplanes. Downloads reset each month.

Does the 14-day trial really cost nothing? +

Yes. Base tier gives you 14 full days of unlimited access at no charge. During the trial, you have access to everything: all meditations, all categories, no ads. Your card is charged only after 14 days. Cancel anytime during the trial and you won't be charged.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime? +

Absolutely. Upgrade to a higher tier anytime and you'll be charged a prorated amount. Downgrade anytime and we'll credit your account. Changes take effect immediately. Your meditation history always stays with you.

What happens if I cancel? +

You can cancel anytime with one click. No questions asked. Your access continues until the end of your current billing cycle. If you subscribed to Base ($20), you get access through your full month. No early termination fees.

Do you refund partial months? +

No. But since you can cancel anytime, you only pay for the time you use. If you subscribe to Pro on the 15th and cancel on the 20th, you keep access until the 15th of next month.

Which tier should I choose? +

Free: Try Pure first (30 min/month, ad-supported). Base: Unlimited access without ads, includes 14-day free trial. Pro: Offline downloads and exclusive meditations. Premium: Maximum access — 200 downloads/month, exclusive tracks, and priority support.
