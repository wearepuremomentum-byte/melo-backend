What Is 432Hz?

432Hz is a pure tone frequency — a sound vibrating at 432 cycles per second. In music, it's sometimes called "Verdi's A" because composer Giuseppe Verdi advocated for tuning concert A to 432Hz rather than the modern standard of 440Hz.

432Hz vs 440Hz: What's Actually Different

Modern music is tuned to A440 — the A above middle C vibrates at 440Hz. This has been the international standard since 1953. Before that, tuning varied widely across different countries and eras.

The claim that 432Hz sounds "more natural" has a mathematical basis. 432Hz is mathematically consistent with the Pythagorean tuning system and produces harmonics that align closely with natural ratios found in nature. 440Hz, by contrast, produces slightly different harmonic relationships that some researchers believe create subtle dissonance.

More practically: studies measuring physiological responses to music played at 432Hz versus 440Hz have found that 432Hz versions produce lower heart rates, reduced anxiety scores on self-report measures, and increased reported feelings of warmth and openness in participants.

The Heart Coherence Connection

The most compelling research around 432Hz involves heart rate variability (HRV) — the variation in time between heartbeats that serves as a key indicator of nervous system health. High HRV is associated with resilience, emotional regulation, and longevity. Low HRV is associated with stress, anxiety, and cardiovascular risk.

Listening to tones in the 432Hz range has been associated with increased HRV in multiple studies. The mechanism proposed involves the vagus nerve — the main nerve of the parasympathetic nervous system — responding to specific frequency inputs. The vagus nerve has both auditory and cardiac branches, creating a direct pathway between what you hear and how your heart beats.

How to Use 432Hz Meditation Effectively

Getting results from 432Hz meditation requires a specific approach:

  1. Use headphones — The frequency effects work through both ears simultaneously. Speakers reduce the precision of the frequency delivery

  3. Use it for emotional and heart-related goals — 432Hz is most effective for anxiety, grief, relationship stress, and emotional healing rather than focus or energy
  4. Layer with binaural beats — 432Hz as a carrier frequency underneath Theta binaural beats (6Hz) creates a compound effect: the binaural beats shift your brainwave state while 432Hz supports heart coherence simultaneously
  5. Be consistent — Daily use for 7–14 days produces more reliable results than occasional sessions
