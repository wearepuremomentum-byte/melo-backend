It is a unique identifier for every sound recording (like an ISBN for books). Format: `US-S1Z-26-00001` - **Country (2 letters):** US = United States - **Registrant (3 letters):** Your unique code (e.g., S1Z = assigned to you) - **Year (2 digits):** 26 = 2026 - **Designation (5 digits):** 00001 = sequential track number ## Why ISRCs Are Critical | Platform | Uses ISRC For | |----------|---------------| | **BMI** | Identifies your work for royalty collection | | **SoundExchange** | The ONLY identifier they use for digital performance royalties | | **RouteNote** | Submits ISRC to all platforms (Spotify, Apple, etc.) | | **Spotify** | Links all streams to your artist profile | | **YouTube Content ID** | Matches audio fingerprint to your ISRC | | **Royalty collection** | All PROs and collection societies use ISRC to track plays | **Without ISRCs:** You cannot collect SoundExchange royalties, and platforms may not properly attribute streams to you. ## How to Get ISRCs **Option A: Through RouteNote (FREE)** - RouteNote automatically assigns ISRCs when you upload - You can view them in your dashboard after upload - Copy them into `TRACK_METADATA_REGISTRY.md` **Option B: Direct from ISRC Agency (USA)** - Go to https://usisrc.org (US ISRC Agency) - Register as a registrant (free) - Get your unique 3-letter registrant code - Generate your own ISRCs using the format: `US-[YOURCODE]-26-[00001-00037]` **Option C: Through your PRO (BMI)** - BMI can generate ISRCs for members - Contact BMI member services after your account is approved **Recommended:** Use RouteNote's free ISRCs, then copy them into all your registrations. --- # PART 5: THE COMPLETE TRACK METADATA REGISTRY See `TRACK_METADATA_REGISTRY.md` for the complete table of all 37 tracks with: -
