Calm's downloads have fallen 61% since 2018. Headspace has lost 74% of its download momentum. Meanwhile, 15 to 20 million people search for alternatives every single month.

What People Are Actually Saying

We analyzed thousands of App Store reviews and Reddit threads from users who cancelled Calm and Headspace. The complaints fall into clear categories:

Calm's Top Complaints

  • Billing issues (35% of negative reviews) — Users reporting charges after cancellation, hidden auto-renewals, and difficulty actually stopping payments
  • Price (30%) — At $70/year or $17/month, users feel they're not getting value proportional to cost
  • Feature bloat (25%) — "I just want to meditate, not use a social app" appears in hundreds of reviews
  • Content repetition (20%) — The same sleep stories and voices become boring after a few months

"I can't feel any difference after using Calm for 6 months. I'm just listening to stories. Where's the actual science?"

Headspace's Core Problem

  • Users describe it as "a school course, not meditation"
  • No mechanism for immediate anxiety relief — everything is a multi-day program
  • No science-backed frequency component in any track
  • Repetitive animations that feel corporate and disconnected

The Science Gap: What Both Apps Are Missing

Here's what the research actually shows about effective meditation: your brain responds measurably to specific audio frequencies. This is called brainwave entrainment, and it has been studied extensively in peer-reviewed research.

Binaural Beats

Play 103 Hz in the left ear and 109 Hz in the right, and the brain perceives a 6 Hz Theta wave — shifting your brain into a measurable state of deep relaxation.

Solfeggio Frequencies

396 Hz, 432 Hz, 528 Hz — specific frequencies associated with emotional release, heart coherence, and cellular vitality. Used in healing traditions for centuries, now studied scientifically.

40 Hz Gamma

Published research from MIT shows 40 Hz gamma oscillations improve focus, working memory, and cognitive performance. This is the frequency your brain runs at during peak mental states.

Delta Waves (0.5–4 Hz)

The brain naturally produces delta waves during deep sleep. Binaural beats at these frequencies can help guide the brain into restorative sleep states faster and more reliably.

Neither Calm nor Headspace uses any of this. Their content is narrated stories and ambient music — pleasant, but with no mechanism for measurable brain state change. This is why users report "not feeling any difference."
