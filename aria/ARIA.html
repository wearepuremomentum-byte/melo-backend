# STRIPE & PAYPAL SETUP — PURE MEDITATION ## Complete Step-by-Step Guide --- # PART 1: STRIPE SETUP ## Estimated Time: 20 minutes --- ## STEP 1 — CREATE YOUR STRIPE ACCOUNT 1. Go to **https://stripe.com** 2. Click **"Start now"** 3. Enter your email + create password 4. Verify your email (check inbox for confirmation link) 5. Log in to your Stripe Dashboard --- ## STEP 2 — COMPLETE ACCOUNT ACTIVATION (Required to receive money) 1. In Stripe Dashboard, click **"Activate your account"** (top of screen) 2. Fill out: - **Business type:** Individual or Company (Individual if you're solo) - **Business name:** Pure Meditation (or wearemomentum) - **Business website:** https://wearemomentum.netlify.app - **Industry:** Software / SaaS / Online Services - **Business address:** Your Kalamazoo address - **Phone number:** Your number 3. Add your **bank account** for payouts: - Click **"Add bank account"** - Enter your routing + account number 4. Add your **SSN** (last 4 digits for individual) 5. Click **Submit** > ⚠️ You can get API keys immediately — you don't need to wait for activation to start testing. --- ## STEP 3 — GET YOUR API KEYS 1. In the left sidebar, click **"Developers"** 2. Click **"API keys"** 3. You'll see two keys: ``` Publishable key: pk_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx Secret key: sk_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx ``` 4. Click **"Reveal live key"** next to the Secret key 5. **Copy the Secret key** — you'll put this in Railway > ⚠️ NEVER put the Secret key in your HTML/frontend code. Backend only. --- ## STEP 4 — CREATE YOUR PRODUCTS (3 plans) This is important — create a Product for each tier you sell, then create Prices under each Product for monthly billing. ### Create Base Plan ($20/month): 1. In left sidebar → **"Product catalog"** 2. Click **"+ Add product"** 3. Fill in: - **Name:** Pure Meditation — Base - **Description:** Unlimited meditation, no ads, 14-day free trial - **Image:** (optional — upload your logo) 4. Under **Pricing**, set: - **Pricing model:** Standard pricing - **Price:** `20.00` - **Currency:** USD - **Billing period:** Monthly 5. Click **"Save product"** 6. **Copy the Price ID** — looks like `price_1Abc...` — save it somewhere ### Create Pro Plan ($29.99/month): 1. Click **"+ Add product"** again 2. Fill in: - **Name:** Pure Meditation — Pro - **Description:** Unlimited + 50 offline downloads + exclusive tracks 3. Under **Pricing**: - **Price:** `29.99` - **Currency:** USD - **Billing period:** Monthly 4. Click **"Save product"** 5. **Copy the Price ID** ### Create Premium Plan ($49.99/month): 1. Click **"+ Add product"** again 2. Fill in: - **Name:** Pure Meditation — Premium - **Description:** Unlimited + 200 downloads + 4 coaching sessions/month 3. Under **Pricing**: - **Price:** `49.99` - **Currency:** USD - **Billing period:** Monthly 4. Click **"Save product"** 5. **Copy the Price ID** > You should now have 3 Price IDs saved. They look like: `price_1Abc123XYZ` --- ## STEP 5 — SET UP WEBHOOK (So Stripe tells your backend when someone pays) Navigate to Developers > Webhooks and click Add endpoint. 1. In left sidebar → **"Developers"** → **"Webhooks"** 2. Click **"+ Add endpoint"** 3. **Endpoint URL:** `https://melo-backend-production-b12f.up.railway.app/webhook` 4. Under **"Select events"**, add these events: - `checkout.session.completed` - `customer.subscription.deleted` - `customer.subscription.updated` - `invoice.payment_failed` 5. Click **"Add endpoint"** 6. Click on the webhook you just created 7. Click **"Reveal"** next to **Signing secret** 8. **Copy the Signing secret** — looks like `whsec_xxxxxxxxxxxx` --- ## STEP 6 — ADD KEYS TO RAILWAY Go to Railway → your backend project → **Variables** tab Add these 3 environment variables: ``` STRIPE_SECRET_KEY = sk_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET = whsec_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx FRONTEND_URL = https://wearemomentum.netlify.app ``` Click **Deploy** after adding variables. --- ## STEP 7 — TEST STRIPE BEFORE GOING LIVE 1. In Stripe Dashboard, toggle **"Test mode"** (top left — flip the switch) 2. In Test mode, your test API keys look like `sk_test_...` 3. Use test card number: **4242 4242 4242 4242** - Expiry: any future date (e.g., 12/28) - CVV: any 3 digits (e.g., 123) - ZIP: any 5 digits (e.g., 10001) 4. Go to your pricing page and click "Try Free for 14 Days" 5. Complete a test checkout 6. Check Stripe Dashboard → **"Payments"** — you should see a test charge 7. Check your Railway backend logs — should show webhook received > When everything works in test mode, switch back to **Live mode** and replace test keys with live keys in Railway. --- ## STRIPE DONE ✅ **What you now have:** - Stripe account activated - 3 subscription products created ($20 / $29.99 / $49.99) - Webhook configured - Keys added to Railway - Your checkout button redirects to Stripe's hosted page (handles Card + Apple Pay + Google Pay automatically) --- --- # PART 2: PAYPAL SETUP ## Estimated Time: 20 minutes --- ## STEP 1 — CREATE A PAYPAL BUSINESS ACCOUNT > Skip this if you already have a PayPal business account 1.
