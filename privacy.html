🔒 Privacy Policy

How we protect your data and respect your privacy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

Pure Meditation ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") operates the Pure Meditation app and website located at https://wearemomentum.netlify.app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices in detail.

By using Pure Meditation, you consent to our data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Email address and password when creating an account
  • Subscription Information: Tier selected (Free, Base, Pro, Premium) and subscription preferences
  • Payment Information: Processed via Stripe—we never store credit card details on our servers
  • Contact Information: Name, email, and message content when you contact support
  • Feedback: Comments, suggestions, and feedback about Pure Meditation

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Meditation History: Which meditations you play, frequency, duration completed, and time spent
  • Device Information: Device type (iPhone, Android, etc), operating system version, browser type
  • Usage Data: Features accessed, buttons clicked, pages viewed, time spent in app
  • Technical Information: IP address, internet service provider, referring URL, pages visited
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address (not GPS-based)

2.3 Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in securely
  • Remember your preferences
  • Track anonymous usage patterns for improvement
  • Prevent fraud and enhance security

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Creating and maintaining your account, tracking progress, syncing across devices
  • Payment Processing: Processing subscriptions and payments via Stripe
  • Improvement: Analyzing usage patterns to improve Pure Meditation features and meditations
  • Communications: Sending service updates, billing notices, and support responses
  • Support: Providing customer support and resolving technical issues
  • Security: Detecting fraud, preventing abuse, and ensuring app security
  • Legal Compliance: Complying with legal obligations and regulations
  • Analytics: Understanding how users interact with our app to optimize experience

4. Data Security & Protection

Your data security is our priority:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted to our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt, not stored in plain text
  • Server Security: Data stored on Railway (our backend provider) with enterprise-grade security
  • Access Controls: Only authorized team members access personal data
  • Payment Security: Payment data processed by Stripe and never stored on our servers
  • Regular Audits: We conduct security audits and vulnerability assessments
Important: While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication if available.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We use trusted third-party services that handle data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Stripe: Payment processing (https://stripe.com/privacy)
  • Railway: Backend hosting and database (https://railway.app/privacy)
  • Netlify: Frontend hosting (https://www.netlify.com/privacy/)
  • Suno AI: Music generation (https://www.suno.ai/privacy)
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous usage analytics

We never sell your personal data to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Third-party services are bound by confidentiality agreements.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Meditation History: Retained for tracking progress and analytics
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years (legal/tax requirement)
  • Support Communications: Retained for 3 years
  • Deleted Accounts: Personal data deleted within 30 days of account deletion (except where required by law)

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Portability: Export your meditation history in a readable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails anytime
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@puremeditation.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Pure Meditation is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information should contact us immediately at privacy@puremeditation.com.

9. International Data Transfers

Pure Meditation operates in the United States. Your data may be stored or processed in the US or other locations where our service providers operate. By using Pure Meditation, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your country of residence, which may have different data protection rules.

10. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are located in the European Union, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Your rights under GDPR include all rights mentioned in Section 7, plus:

  • Data Protection Officer: Contact us at privacy@puremeditation.com
  • Lawful Basis: We process data based on your consent (subscription) and legitimate interests (service improvement)
  • Complaints: You may lodge complaints with your local data protection authority

11. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Know: What personal information we collect
  • Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Opt-out: Opt-out of personal information sales (we don't sell data)
  • Non-Discrimination: No discrimination for exercising privacy rights

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes, we'll notify you by updating the "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, we'll send a notice to your email or display a prominent notice in the app. Continued use of Pure Meditation after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
