Everything you need to know about Pure Meditation

What is Pure Meditation? −

Pure Meditation is a simple, beautiful meditation app with 13 carefully curated meditations across 5 categories: Sleep, Focus, Anxiety, Energy, and Fitness. We believe simplicity works better than complexity. Instead of 1000 options causing decision paralysis, we offer the best meditation for each situation. No complicated features. No sleep stories. No coaching upsells. Just pure meditation that works.

Is Pure Meditation free? +

Yes! Pure Meditation offers a free tier with 30 minutes of meditation per month. This gives you 3-4 full meditations to try every month. If you love it, upgrade to unlimited access. Our paid tiers are $20-50/month depending on features like offline downloads and exclusive tracks.

Free: $0/month, 30 minutes per month, ad-supported, all categories access.

Base: $20/month, unlimited meditation, no ads, 14-day free trial, all categories.

Pro: $29.99/month, unlimited meditation, 50 offline downloads per month, exclusive Pro meditations, all categories.

What's new about the Fitness category? +

Our Fitness category has 4 subcategories designed for different workout phases:

Pre-Workout Focus: Uses beta/gamma frequencies for alertness and mental intensity before lifting, running, or competing. Prepares your nervous system for peak performance.

During Workout: Repetitive rhythmic sounds for pacing, concentration, and getting in the zone—similar to workout music but optimized for meditation and focus.

Breathwork & Cardio: Breathing guidance for steady-state cardio, yoga, mobility work, and martial arts. Helps regulate breathing and movement rhythm.

Recovery & Cooldown: Calming soundscapes to lower stress hormones and shift from high-adrenaline back to recovery mode after intense training.

Can I download meditations for offline use? +

Yes! With our Pro tier, you can download 50 meditations per month offline. With Premium, you get 200 downloads per month. This is perfect for workouts, travel, or places without reliable internet. Free and Base tiers require internet connection.

How long are the meditations? +

Meditations range from 5 minutes (Energy Boost) to 60 minutes (Sleep Ocean). Most are 10-30 minutes. You can choose your duration based on your schedule:

• 5-10 min: Perfect for quick morning boosts or work breaks

• 15-20 min: Ideal for most situations and commutes

• 30+ min: Deep immersion for sleep and extended meditation

Do I need to create an account? +

Pure Meditation works on iPhone, Android, iPad, Mac, Windows, and any device with a web browser. It's a web app, so you don't need to download from app stores. You can also add it to your home screen to make it feel like a native app. Log in from any device and your progress syncs automatically.
